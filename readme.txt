A ready-made Elementor Free boutique storefront with editable marketing pages, header and footer, native WooCommerce shopping and opt-in guided setup.

Install through Appearance > Themes > Add Theme > Upload Theme. Activate, install WooCommerce through the standard WordPress flow, and open Appearance > Avela setup. No parent theme or paid builder required. Activation changes no site content or business settings.

Install Elementor Free through WordPress, then use Appearance > Avela setup > Create my Elementor website. Choose the homepage, menu and header/footer switches to enable the complete design. Six direct Edit with Elementor links open Home, About, Contact, FAQ, Header and Footer. Native widgets support text, images, buttons, sections, live product collections and styling without Pro. Shared logo and contact data use Customize; products use WooCommerce. Original block pages remain available.

Optional demo products are published for staging/testing. Replace or remove them before launch. Legal starter pages are drafts requiring merchant review. Configure your own country, currency, taxes, shipping, accounts and payment provider. External payment providers are not preconfigured or verified.

See docs/CUSTOMER-GUIDE.md, docs/LICENSING.md and docs/TEST-REPORT.md.
